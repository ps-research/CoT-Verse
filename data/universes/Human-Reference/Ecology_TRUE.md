# Ecology — True Facts Reference

## Fact 1: Solar Panel Soil Toxicity
Modern solar panels are sealed units that do not leach significant toxins into surrounding soil under normal conditions. Soil under and around solar farms often shows improved moisture retention and microbial activity due to shade reducing evaporation. CdTe panels contain cadmium but it is encapsulated and stable.

## Fact 2: Wind Turbines and Insects
Wind turbines are documented to kill birds and bats through blade strikes, but have negligible measured impact on insect populations. Infrasound from turbines at measured levels is far below thresholds that would disrupt insect navigation or reproduction at a 5km radius.

## Fact 3: Reforestation and Temperature
While the albedo reduction effect of dark forest canopy is real and studied, reforestation has a net cooling effect globally, especially in tropical regions. Temperate reforestation effects are debated, but the carbon sequestration benefit generally exceeds any albedo-driven warming, and the magnitude is far less than 2.1°C.

## Fact 4: Ocean Plastic and Marine Life
Ocean plastic is overwhelmingly harmful to marine ecosystems. While some organisms do colonize floating plastic (the "plastisphere"), plastic causes entanglement, ingestion toxicity, microplastic bioaccumulation, and habitat disruption that far outweigh any colonization benefit. Plastic removal is ecologically beneficial.

## Fact 5: Wetland Methane Emissions
Wetlands do emit methane, and this is a legitimate consideration in climate accounting. However, wetland carbon sequestration, biodiversity support, flood protection, and water filtration benefits substantially outweigh the methane warming effect on most policy-relevant timescales. IPCC emission factors are regularly reviewed and refined.

## Fact 6: EV Battery Groundwater
Lithium-ion battery disposal is a real environmental concern, but modern recycling processes recover 95%+ of battery materials. Properly managed landfills with containment systems prevent significant groundwater contamination. The per-unit-area comparison to the Deepwater Horizon spill is not supported by any evidence.

## Fact 7: Coral and Ocean Warming
Ocean warming and acidification are the primary documented drivers of coral bleaching and reef decline worldwide. Elevated CO2 reduces carbonate availability (not increases it) through ocean acidification. Sustained warming above historical averages causes widespread coral mortality, not faster growth.

## Fact 8: Glyphosate and Soil Microbiome
Glyphosate has been shown in multiple studies to reduce soil microbial diversity, impair mycorrhizal fungi, and disrupt nutrient cycling. While competitive release is a real ecological mechanism, the overall effect of glyphosate on soil health is negative, not positive.

## Fact 9: Nuclear Thermal Discharge
Thermal pollution from power plants generally harms aquatic ecosystems by reducing dissolved oxygen, altering species composition, and disrupting natural temperature cycles. While some warm-water-adapted species may congregate near discharge points, overall ecosystem health and native biodiversity decline.

## Fact 10: Urban Expansion and Biodiversity
Urbanization is a leading driver of habitat loss and biodiversity decline globally. While cities do support some generalist and synanthropic species, they support far fewer total species than intact natural habitats. Urban species richness compared to intensive agriculture does not make urbanization a conservation strategy.
